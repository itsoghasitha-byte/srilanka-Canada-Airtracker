# Private jet charter: beginner guide

Charter costs vary widely by aircraft class, route, availability, repositioning, and airport fees. Treat online prices as rough estimates, not quotes.

## How to request a quote
1. Send the same route, dates, and passenger count to multiple reputable brokers or operators.
2. Ask for an all-in quote including taxes, handling, and aircraft positioning.
3. Verify the operator's air operator certificate, insurance, and independent safety credentials.
4. Read the contract carefully and use secure, traceable payment methods.
5. Ask whether an empty-leg flight fits your route and schedule.

This tracker displays aggregate public aircraft data only. It does not identify aircraft owners and should not be used to follow private individuals.
