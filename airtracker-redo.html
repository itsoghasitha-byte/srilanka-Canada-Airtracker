Seat belt
Keep it low, snug and fastened whenever seated.
Exits
Find your nearest exits. One may be behind you.
Oxygen mask
Pull one toward you, cover nose and mouth, then help others.
Life vest
Read the safety card. Inflate only when crew instruct or after leaving the aircraft.
Turbulence
Return to your seat, buckle up and secure loose items.
Evacuation
Leave bags behind and follow crew commands.

General guidance only. Follow the aircraft safety card and the operating crew.
